About this archive

The Crimson Archive is a projected garden installation of red flowers — a field of blossoms that stand in for the blood spilled, and the courage spent, in the fight for Korea's independence during the Japanese colonial period. Red, here, is not only mourning. It is also the color of something alive: conviction, resistance, and a freedom that had to be fought for.

This website is the archive's companion — a place to keep the stories behind the flowers, so that the installation points to something real. Each entry below is a documented person or event from that history. It is a living archive: new stories are added over time.
